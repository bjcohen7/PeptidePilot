## 3 Actionable Optimization Areas

### A. Content → Organic Traffic (Highest Upside)
Google organic = only 6 visits/week (3%). The 186 new batch-4 pSEO pages just launched:
- 37 guides, 55 reviews, 56 conditions, 16 stacks, 10 goals, 12 comparisons
- Before this launch, Google had ~50 pages to index. Now it has ~630.
- If SEO is successful over 60-90 days, this could 3-5x organic traffic.

**Action:** Ensure new pages have internal links to the quiz (most do via bottom CTA). Monitor Google Search Console for impressions/clicks on new pages after 2-4 weeks.

### B. Affiliate Link Optimization (Medium Effort, Fast Win)

**1. Add merchandising to ALL existing links**
Every active link is missing promo text, coupon codes, badges, and headline values. Even something simple like showing "Save $50" or "Free Shipping" would likely lift click-through. This is a data-entry fix in the admin panel.

**2. Fix paused links for top-matching peptides**
- **bpc157**: 1 active, 5 paused. Unpause or replace the 5 paused.
- **ipamorelin_cjc1295**: 1 active, 5 paused. Same issue.
- **selank_semax**: 2 active, 4 paused.
- **Check why so many links are paused** — the audit log shows 169 events, possibly bulk pauses.

**3. Add partners for single-point-of-failure peptides**
- **ipamorelin_cjc1295** (31% of leads) needs a 2nd partner beyond Limitless Biotech
- **bpc157** (10% of leads, 88% aff click rate) urgently needs a 2nd partner — this audience converts
- Reach out to SkinnyRX, PeterMD, Direct Meds for these

**4. Activate or replace epithalon, tb500, pt141**
These have matches but ZERO active links. Either negotiate with current partners or add new ones.

### C. Results Page Conversion (Medium Effort)

51% of results viewers don't click any affiliate link. Possible causes:
- Results page presents too many choices → decision paralysis
- Affiliate links look generic without pricing/promo context
- Users want to research more before buying

**Action:**
- Add promo badges to links ("Best price", "Popular", "Free consult")
- Test showing 1-2 featured offers vs 3-4
- Add price anchoring if partners share pricing data
